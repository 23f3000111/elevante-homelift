The system

A lift inside the staircase

Elevante is not a lift placed beside your stairs. The cabin travels vertically inside the staircase footprint, behind the flight, between the ground floor and the first floor.

SafetyMovement detection / automatic doors

Safety is part of the mechanism, not an accessory

The system detects movement on and around the staircase. If movement is detected the lift will not start, and if movement begins while the cabin is travelling it stops.

The doors follow the same logic. The opening at the upper floor is closed whenever the cabin is not there, and the lower opening closes as the cabin rises.

Drawing 03Plan / cabin occupancy

The cabin arrives level with the floor. No step up, no threshold to cross.

The rollator goes in with you. It does not have to be folded or left behind.

The cabin takes a wheelchair, and you do not need to turn it around inside.

There is room for a second person, so a partner, family member or carer travels with you.
